# Design 002 — Objects in Field / 0.2.0 short studio

Status: internally accepted after focused implementation and full-state QA on 2026-09-06; see `docs/qa/002-short-studio.md`. Old 0.1.0 remains rejected. This status does not claim user acceptance.

Purpose: a concise spatial/creative studio homepage template in 2–5 screens, built from the retained design language of https://lusion.co/. Active map: `docs/references/002-short-studio.md`. Prior failed implementations are archived under `docs/history/002-rejected-20260830/`.

Locked direction for the key-state test: pale cool canvas, black neutral grotesk, one ultramarine accent; normal document flow; restrained introduction over a wide original object image; one asymmetrical editorial composition with process imagery; one media-led finale with a compact closing row. No distant dark scene or featured-project catalogue.

Preserve the three chosen original images and their unique roles. Reuse no image twice. Decorative control labels remain inert as required for this case; no audio, wheel interception, scroll snapping, scripted chapter jumps, fixed header changes, WebGL loop or elaborate loading sequence. Pointer framing may be added only after static states pass, and reduced motion keeps complete compositions.

All content/structure incompatible with a 2–5-screen template may be removed or locally adapted. Record adaptations before implementation. Four review states are cover, editorial, adapted transition and ending, not four compulsory full-screen sections. Standard viewports: 2048×912, 1440×900, 390×844; audit 320px width as a compact regression. Customer source: one Vite HTML/CSS/JS archive with only used assets and customization notes.
