A SMALL-GROUP CULINARY EXPERIENCE

What does the day look like?+

We begin with a gentle walk and a guide to seasonal edible plants. Back at the table, we make pasta by hand, cook together and share a long lunch. There is time to learn, ask questions and simply enjoy being outside.

Who can join?+

Curious beginners and experienced home cooks are equally welcome. No previous foraging or pasta-making experience is needed. Let us know about access needs when you enquire.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and a layer for changing weather. Ingredients and cooking tools are provided. Please share any dietary requirements before booking.

How do I arrange a day?+

Send us your preferred dates and group size by email. We will discuss availability, the location and the full price before you commit.
